# Derp Universe Growth & Health Metrics Dashboard

## Core Business Metrics
- DAU (Daily Active Users)
- WAU (Weekly Active Users)
- MAU (Monthly Active Users)
- DAU/MAU Ratio (Stickiness)

### DAU/MAU Benchmarks
- <15%: Weak
- 20–30%: Healthy
- 30–40%: Strong
- 40%+: Exceptional
- Idle game target: 20–35%

---

## Retention Metrics

### D1 Retention
- Poor: <25%
- Average: 30–40%
- Good: 40–50%
- Excellent: 50%+

### D7 Retention
- Poor: <10%
- Good: 15–20%
- Excellent: 25%+

### D30 Retention
- Poor: <3%
- Good: 5–10%
- Excellent: 10–15%

---

## Engagement Metrics

### Average Session Length
Target: 10–30 minutes

### Sessions Per Day
Target: 2–5 sessions/day

### Mission Participation Rate
Players Running Missions ÷ Active Players

### Outpost Collection Rate
Collected Resources ÷ Potential Resources Generated

---

## Progression Health Metrics

### Level Distribution
Monitor player distribution across level ranges and identify progression cliffs.

### Ascension Funnel
Track:
Level 50 → T1 → T2 → T3 → T4 → T5

### Time To First Ascension
Monitor median days required to reach T1.

### Team Diversity
Track average archetype diversity used in missions.

---

## Economy Metrics

### Arcanium Faucet vs Sink Ratio
Generated ÷ Spent

Ideal:
- 1.0–1.2 ratio

### Material Inflation
Track:
- Created
- Consumed
- Destroyed

For:
- Common
- Advanced
- Rare
- Ultra-Rare

### Essence Velocity
Track:
- Essence Earned
- Essence Spent

### Resource Hoarding Index
Median days of resources held.

---

## Seasonal Metrics

### Season Participation
Season Participants ÷ DAU

### Battle Pass Completion Rate
Completed Pass ÷ Started Pass

Healthy target:
- 40–60%

### Seasonal Boss Participation
Boss Participants ÷ Season Participants

### Seasonal Retention Lift
Retention During Season vs Retention Outside Season

---

## Monetization Metrics

### Conversion Rate
Paying Users ÷ Active Users

Benchmarks:
- Average: 1–3%
- Good: 3–5%
- Excellent: 5%+

### ARPDAU
Revenue ÷ DAU

### Battle Pass Attach Rate
Premium Pass Buyers ÷ Season Participants
